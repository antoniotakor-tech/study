Where to put it

  • Next to the bins. The one place people are already thinking about the question. Eye height, not floor height.
  • At the canteen exit. Catches people holding the packaging the survey asks about.
  • In your class channel. Paste the link rather than the QR — a code inside a screen is one scan too many.
  • Ask, don't post. Handing your phone to someone gets a response rate a poster never will.

Check your school's rules before putting posters up. Most buildings have designated boards and will remove anything else — ask the facilities desk, and mention it's for a course project.
