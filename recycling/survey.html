What happens to your answers

Responses are collected for a student research project and reported only in aggregate — no individual answer is published or shared. Nothing identifying is asked for: no name, no student number, and no email unless you type one in yourself to join the pilot. An email address given here is used only to contact you about the pilot and is deleted when the project is graded. The site sets no tracking cookies and no analytics. If you want your response removed afterwards, contact the researcher and it will be deleted.

The three answers

Greasy pizza box
Residual. Grease and cheese contaminate the paper stream — a soaked box ruins the batch it's mixed into. Clean cardboard lid, dirty base: tear them apart.
Drink carton
PMD. Cartons look like paper but are layered with plastic and aluminium, so they're collected with plastic and metal packaging.
Plastic fork
Residual. PMD takes plastic packaging only. Cutlery, toys and other plastic objects aren't packaging, so they don't belong there.
